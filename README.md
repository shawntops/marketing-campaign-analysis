# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes **308 digital marketing campaign records** to evaluate how different campaigns performed across the marketing funnel, from impressions and clicks to leads, orders, and revenue.

The goal was to move beyond surface-level engagement metrics and identify which campaigns actually produced stronger commercial results.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which campaigns generated the most revenue?
- Which campaigns delivered the strongest return on advertising spend?
- Which campaigns produced the highest click-through rates?
- How efficiently did campaigns convert clicks into leads and orders?
- Does stronger engagement necessarily translate into stronger commercial performance?

The dataset contains campaign-level performance data including:

**Records analyzed:** 308

## Key Metrics

The following KPIs were calculated to evaluate campaign performance:

| Metric | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | Measures how effectively impressions generated clicks |
| Lead Conversion Rate | Measures how effectively clicks generated leads |
| Order Conversion Rate | Measures progression from leads/clicks toward completed orders |
| Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) | Measures revenue generated for each unit of marketing spend |
| Cost per Click (CPC) | Measures the cost of generating a click |
| Cost per Lead (CPL) | Measures the cost of acquiring a lead |

## Analysis Process

1. Reviewed and prepared the campaign dataset for analysis.
2. Created calculated performance metrics for engagement, conversion, and advertising efficiency.
3. Used PivotTables to summarize performance across campaigns and categories.
4. Built an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers for easier exploration.
5. Compared engagement metrics with revenue and ROAS to identify meaningful performance differences.

## Key Findings

### Overall Performance

- **Total Revenue:** approximately **₦42.89M**
- **Total Marketing Spend:** approximately **₦30.59M**
- **Overall ROAS:** approximately **1.40x**

### Influencer Marketing

Influencer campaigns were the strongest category by commercial return:

- **Revenue:** approximately **₦21.12M**
- **Marketing Spend:** approximately **₦8.31M**
- **ROAS:** approximately **2.54x**

### YouTube Blogger

The **YouTube Blogger** campaign emerged as the strongest individual campaign in the analysis:

- **Revenue:** approximately **₦15.31M**
- **Marketing Spend:** approximately **₦4.06M**
- **Orders:** **1,914**
- **ROAS:** approximately **3.77x**

### Engagement vs Commercial Performance

**Facebook Retargeting** recorded the highest click-through rate at approximately **3.07%**.

However, the campaign with the strongest CTR was not the campaign with the strongest revenue or ROAS.

This highlights an important distinction: **high engagement does not automatically translate into the strongest commercial performance.**

## Business Insight

The analysis showed that campaign effectiveness varied significantly depending on the metric used.

While Facebook Retargeting achieved the strongest click-through performance, influencer campaigns generated the highest overall revenue and return on advertising spend. YouTube Blogger was particularly effective, producing approximately ₦15.31M in revenue and a 3.77x ROAS.

This suggests that campaign decisions should consider the entire conversion funnel rather than relying on engagement metrics such as CTR alone.
